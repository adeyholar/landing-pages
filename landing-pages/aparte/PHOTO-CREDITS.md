## Use in this fictional design study

The photographs illustrate the gift-delivery concept. The people pictured
are stock models, not Aparté customers, employees or endorsers. Flensburg
is a real place used as visual inspiration for the fictional Larch Harbor.

The bouquet is also used inside the HTML app illustration. The cover photo
is used in `app/opengraph-image.png` and the Aparté preview on the collection
site. Those composites retain the photography credits above. Logos, QR
codes, code-drawn storefronts and gift cards are original project assets.
